Tickets

**1. Online Real-Name Reservation:**  
The maximum daily visitor capacity is 85,000. All visitors are required to make an online real-name reservations and present the original valid ID document used for that reservation upon entry. Seniors aged 60 and above, and children under 6 years of age or under 1.3 meters in height are exempt from reservation and may enter the Base with their original valid ID documents.  
**2. Opening Hours:**  
November to February: Morning Entry: 8:00 – 12:00; Afternoon Entry: 12:00 – 16:30. Park clearance begins at 17:30.   
March to October: Morning Entry: 7:30 – 12:00; Afternoon Entry: 12:00 – 17:00. Park clearance begins at 18:00.  
**3. Reservation Time:**  
Tickets may be reserved online up to 14 days in advance. Sales end when the daily ticket quota is sold out. Visitors with afternoon tickets should not arrive early to avoid long waits.  
**4. Reservation Channels:**  
Please reserve tickets through the official WeChat account, WeChat mini program, or officially authorized platforms: Meituan APP, Trip.com APP, Douyin APP, Tongcheng Travel APP and“Chengdu Scenic Shuttle Bus” official WeChat account.

**5.Refund Policy:**  
Tickets are valid only on the scheduled visit date and expire after that date. No refunds will be issued once tickets are used! For unused tickets, you may apply for a refund through the original purchase channel within 30 days starting at 5:00 PM on the day of your visit. No refunds will be granted after this period, and the tickets will be automatically canceled by the system.  
**6. Invoice Issuance:**  
If you need an invoice after using your ticket, please apply through your original purchase channel. For visitors who purchased tickets through the official WeChat mini program, please click "Me" > "Issue Invoice" within one year from the date of visit (please note the invoicing deadline; invoicing will be unavailable after the deadline!). Fill in the relevant invoice information and apply for the invoice yourself. The e-invoice will be sent to the email address you provided within 7 business days after application.  
**7. Important Notes:**  
Please refrain from purchasing tickets through any unofficial channels. Each ticket is valid for one person only and must correspond to a single valid ID. Entry is prohibited if ID information does not match ticket details. Beware of any paid “fast-track entry” services.

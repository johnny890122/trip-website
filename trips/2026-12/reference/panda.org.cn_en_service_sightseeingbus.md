Sightseeing Bus Service

**Ticket Purchase Information:  
1. Ticket Sales Hours:**  
(1) Summer (March-October): 7:30 - 17:30, service ends at 18:00;  
(2) Winter (November-February of the following year): 8:00 - 17:00, service ends at 17:30.  
  
**2. Ticket Rules:**  
(1) Full-price Ticket: CNY 30 per person. Must be purchased with real-name registration. Valid only for the ticket holder on the day of visit; passengers may board unlimited times at any stop upon presentation of the original ID card or the purchase QR code;  
(2) Monthly Pass: CNY 298 per person per month. Must be purchased online with real-name registration. Valid for one month, non-transferable and valid only for the registered user; the original ID card is required for boarding;  
(3) Children aged 6 or under or measuring 1.3 m or less in height ride for free (each adult may bring only one free-admission child, who does not occupy a separate seat);  
(4) Unauthorized use of tickets is strictly prohibited. Any violation will result in immediate ticket invalidation and handling in accordance with regulations.  
  
**3. Ticket Purchase Channels:**  
(1) Official Channels: the official mini program or Official WeChat Account of Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding (reservations may be made up to 14 days in advance), or on-site purchase on the day of visit.  
(2) Officially Authorized Platforms: Meituan, Douyin, Ctrip and Tongcheng Travel.  
(3) Group Tickets: web-based booking channel for travel agencies/groups:  
https://panda-api.giantpanda.org.cn/api-api/app/clientHelper/pass/h5LoginV2?merchantNo=E888888871  
(Note: To reduce the time spent queuing to buy tickets on site, groups are advised to purchase tickets online in advance.)  
  
**4. Refund Rules:**  
Tickets are valid on the day of your visit and expire thereafter. No refunds will be issued once tickets are used! For unused tickets, please process your refund through the original purchase channel within 30 days starting from 17:00 on the day of your visit. No refunds will be granted after this period, and the tickets will be automatically marked as used by the system. These rules take effect from August 27, 2026, and are not retroactive.  
  
**5. Invoice Issuance:**  
If you need an invoice, please apply through the channel where you purchased your ticket. Visitors who purchased tickets through the official WeChat mini program may, within one year from the date of visit, go to “Me” - “Invoice Request”, fill in the relevant invoice information and submit a self-service request; the e-invoice will be sent to the email address you provided within 7 working days. For tickets purchased through other channels, invoice issuance is subject to the rules of the corresponding platform.  
  
**6. Passenger Information:**  
(1) All Stops  
① South to West: South Gate Departure Station → Panda Villa Stop → Sun Giant Panda Nursery House Stop → Panda Reception Hall Stop → Bamboo Tower Stop → Star Giant Panda Nursery House Stop → Panda Creek Valley Stop → West Gate Terminal Station;  
② West to South: West Gate Departure Station → Panda Creek Valley Stop → Star Giant Panda Nursery House Stop → Bamboo Tower Stop → No.1 Giant Panda Villa Stop → Sun Giant Panda Nursery House Stop → Swan Lake Stop → South Gate Terminal Station;  
(Note: Swan Lake Stop, Panda Villa Stop, No.1 Giant Panda Villa Stop and Panda Reception Hall Stop are one-way stops. Please pay attention to the direction of travel; if you end up at the wrong stop, you may transfer at the next stop.)  
(2) Upon arrival at the South Gate or West Gate Terminal Station, passengers must re-queue and have their tickets checked again if they wish to take another ride;  
(3) To safeguard the welfare of the giant pandas and their habitat, and in light of the park's hilly terrain and operational safety requirements, the sightseeing bus stops are located some distance on foot from the venues. For the specific layout of venues and stops, please refer to the park map or the official mini program.  
  
**7. Important Notes:**  
(1) When the vehicle is in motion, please do not extend your head, hands or feet outside the vehicle. Roughhousing inside the vehicle is strictly prohibited. Please keep all your personal belongings with you when alighting;  
(2) The elderly, children, the sick, the disabled, pregnant women and other priority passengers, as well as patients prone to acute episodes, such as those with hypertension or heart disease, must be accompanied; intoxicated persons are not permitted to board;  
(3) Waiting areas for passengers with special needs are available at the boarding stops. If required, please consult the staff at the stop.
